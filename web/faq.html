Quick help for inviting Radio, starting playback, and keeping music on in your server.

GETTING STARTED

Make Radio part of your server.

Choose a question to see the answer. For anything else, reach us in the support server.

Invite Radio Visit support ↗
01How do I add Radio to my server?

Use the Invite Radio link. You’ll need the Manage Server or Administrator permission in Discord to add a bot.

02How do I get started?

Join a voice channel, then use /play with a song title or supported music link. Browse the command list for playback and queue controls.

03How do I play a song?

Use /play followed by a track link or a search phrase, for example:

/play https://open.spotify.com/track/62PaSfnXSMyLshYJrlTuL3
/play hello
04How do I keep Radio in voice 24/7?

Join the voice channel first, then run /247. Your server may need to meet the bot’s current vote or Premium requirements; see the Premium plans for paid options.
