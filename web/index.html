RADIO / HELP

Questions, answered.

Quick help for inviting Radio, starting playback, and keeping music on in your server.

Visit the full FAQ ↗
01How do I add Radio to my server?

Use the Invite Radio link. You’ll need the Manage Server or Administrator permission in Discord to add a bot.

02How do I get started?

Join a voice channel, then use /play with a song title or supported music link. Browse the command list for playback and queue controls.

03How do I keep Radio in voice 24/7?

Join the voice channel and run /247. Check the Premium plans for available always-on options.
