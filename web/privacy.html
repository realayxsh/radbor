01 / PRIVACYRADIO BOT + WEBSITE

This Privacy Policy explains how Radio handles information when you use the Radio Discord bot, radiomusic.in, and Premium checkout. We process information to provide requested features, maintain server settings, review payment submissions, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information.

Information the bot processes

The bot receives Discord identifiers needed to respond and apply settings, such as user, server, and channel IDs. It may process message text in memory to recognize prefix commands and their arguments. The command logger records command names and source, together with user, server, and channel IDs; it is designed not to record command arguments or message contents.

The bot does not request Discord’s Server Members or Presence intents. Prefix-command message content is requested only when Discord enables the relevant application flag; slash commands are available as an alternative where message-content access is unavailable.

Depending on the features enabled, the bot stores server settings in MongoDB, including command prefixes, 24/7 and autoplay settings, disabled channels, no-prefix access lists, custom server bot icon or banner URLs, Premium entitlements, and Premium codes. The database is operated using the connection configured by the bot owner.

Operational logs and Discord

If enabled by the bot operator, join and leave logs can include server name and ID, member counts, and server-owner details when the bot joins. Music and system logs can include server or channel identifiers and playback or connection events. Error logs are designed to omit raw error stacks and request bodies. These logs are sent to operator-configured Discord webhooks; Discord processes them under its own policies.

Discord login and cookies

When you sign in to the website with Discord, we request your public profile using the identify scope: Discord user ID, username, display name when available, and avatar. We do not receive or ask for your Discord password. The website uses an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax session cookie, marked Secure in production, with a configured lifetime of up to seven days.

Premium payments and submissions

To submit payment proof, the website associates your Discord account with the plan, billing period, payment method, quoted amount, quote reference, submission date, and review status. You may also submit a Discord server ID. UPI proof requires a UTR and screenshot; Litecoin proof requires a public blockchain transaction link. The website stores payment submission records in its configured payment-data directory so they can be shown in your account and reviewed. The current system does not automatically delete these records.

UPI screenshots are held in memory while being forwarded to the private Discord review webhook; the website does not save a screenshot copy to its payment-history file. Discord receives the submitted proof and handles it under its own policies. Never upload passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, or unrelated private documents. Litecoin/INR quotes use CoinGecko’s public price endpoint and do not include your identity or payment details.

Other providers

Radio relies on Discord for bot, login, and webhook features; MongoDB for bot settings; audio providers and Lavalink infrastructure for playback; and CoinGecko for Litecoin price quotes. These providers process information under their own privacy policies. External links are governed by the destination site’s policies.

Retention and privacy requests

Bot settings are retained while needed for the related feature. Some settings are removed when the bot leaves a server, but this does not automatically clear every record. Website payment history remains in the configured data directory until the operator removes it. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact Radio Support or email Radio. Some information may be retained when needed for security, payment disputes, or legal obligations.

Age, changes, and contact

Use of the bot and website is subject to Discord’s minimum age and any higher age requirement under applicable law. We may update this Policy when the service or data practices change; the current version will be posted here. For privacy questions, contact Radio Support or radiobotmanager@gmail.com.
